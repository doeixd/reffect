# Private nested Deferred coordination runtime

Prepared 2026-10-05 before feature edits. Reviewed the existing owner, turn bank, real AsyncContext adapter and generated All coordinator, DGEN/DGR decisions and current helper capture records. Checked the published [Effect 4.0.0 race implementation](https://unpkg.com/effect@4.0.0/src/internal/effect.ts): `race` delegates to `raceAll`, selects the first successful Exit, and its parent exit handler interrupts and awaits remaining fibers. Losing interruption is not the selected race failure.

**DNR-001 — Route checked static subtrees.** Child task identities reserve disjoint contiguous intervals. Group drivers receive each child root and exclusive subtree end, plus their own parent identity. Bank priority reaches a nested waiter through the appropriate child future. Owners and the bank remain invocation-owned fixed storage; no boxed registry, new context field or scalar metadata is introduced.

**DNR-002 — Distinguish aggregate suspension from protocol Pending.** After polling a child, propagate its actual semantic-suspension flag to the group parent only when that child returned Pending. Nested driver retries and armed completion requests alone never acknowledge a resumed waiter prefix. This supports whole-task acknowledgements without interpreting arbitrary Rust Pending as authored suspension.

**DNR-003 — Race selects success and drains losers.** A fixed Race2 coordinator records the first successful child index, signals every other child, and awaits every child's finalization. A losing false/interrupted child cannot erase a selected success; both false outcomes produce interruption in this Never-error profile. Parent cancellation wins at the boundary, including cancellation during the last masked cleanup. Parent watch registration remains independent of bank priority.

**DNR-004 — Nested boundaries preserve ancestor turns.** A completed nested group may leave a turn belonging to an ancestor/completer active; the old global bank-empty assertion is only valid at the outer invocation boundary. Static topology and awaited child completion protect child lifetimes. Trusted host panic or forced Drop remains unsupported.

Validation will compile focused Rust witnesses in debug/release for independent waiter interruption, loser awaited cleanup, nested routing, parent cancellation during masked broadcast and aggregate suspension. Root schedules native tests sequentially; public Deferred admission remains unchanged.

Implementation boundary: the compiler admits at most six contexts, one initial Race2 beneath outer All2/3, Unit child outcomes and Never errors. The runtime adapter can route fixed nested intervals, but this does not establish recursive arbitrary groups, startup inside resumed callbacks, retained typed failures or public admission.

**DNR-005 — Preserve progress through aggregate polling.** An inner driver may consume the bank's shared changed flag. Outer drivers compare bank priority before and after child polling as well as checking the changed flag; a turn routed outside the inner subtree therefore restarts ancestor polling without requiring a new authored suspension. Semantic flags are tracked per just-polled child, cleared for Ready/skipped children, and propagated only for pending children. The current initial-group topology excludes a group ancestor registering itself as a Deferred waiter before entering its nested group; no broader ancestor acknowledgement proof is claimed.

**DNR-006 — Do not start a later synchronous loser.** Official Race stops forking after a synchronous success. Track first polling separately from completion: once a winner exists, skip a later child that never started, while already-started children still receive interruption and finish their installed finalizers. This prevents canceled unstarted Ensuring computations from installing spurious cleanup.

Validation: `CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=0 CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0 vp test packages/reffect/tests/deferred-generated-runtime.test.ts --maxWorkers=1` passes 1/1 (17.02s), compiling Rust debug/release and repeating all witnesses ten times with fresh owners. The independent-waiter fixture runs producer-first and producer-last outer layouts: the Race cancels one parked waiter, awaited cleanup signals a separate Deferred, its producer then completes the original owner, and the surviving waiter receives the value before the producer continuation. Race continuation waits for loser cleanup. Both owner registration lists and the bank drain at the outer boundary. Additional witnesses cover a synchronous winner leaving an unstarted loser unpolled, parent cancellation during loser cleanup, and prior masked broadcast, false watch update and parent/child cleanup cases. Scoped formatting/lint/type checking passes for the three implementation/test files.
