## Decisions before implementation

- **QBUD-001 — Conditional default context.** Use DBUD's default2048 MixedScheduler, default Clock/Tracer, absent effectful hooks, zero inputs and at most one outer Effect.exit observer. Context overrides have no audited bound. Apply this additional check to private generated Queue selection after source-growth and semantic checks. A finite receipt does not enforce ambient context or admit public/RPC Queue. Owned execution, frames and broader operations remain gates.
- **QBUD-002 — Finite retries, not a constant Take guess.** With positive bounded suspend capacity, no end/shutdown or foreign owner operations, each offer occurrence can append one value at most once. A scheduled taker pass requires an appended offer; scheduled passes coalesce. More generally, every failed takeUnsafe observes an empty buffer. A subsequent readiness success or scheduled retry requires a later append. If the retry fails, the buffer is empty again, so another retry needs another distinct append. A released taker is removed before its callback. This argument also covers readiness fast paths after scheduler interleaving and avoids assuming the very no-yield result being proved. Synchronous producer reentry can steal a value, so a Take may retry more than once. Bound each Take by one initial attempt plus the entire invocation's offer-occurrence bound. Charge all child fibers and all Take occurrences, deliberately overcounting mutually incompatible retries. This bounds operation expansion; it does not prove termination of a permanently blocked program.
- **QBUD-003 — Saturating occurrence summaries.** Memoize context-independent summaries, but add their full weight/counters on every incoming edge. Add sequential and All child costs; take independent maxima for mutually exclusive Match branches. Count both offers and takes this way, then charge16 times offers times takes globally. Saturate all arithmetic at2048 and reject reaching that threshold. Reject cycles, unknown operations, other Queue operations and non-All2 groups. Full-edge source growth remains a separate preflight and includes unused alternatives.
- **QBUD-004 — Primitive receipts.** Reuse DBUD's entry24, Succeed5, Map7/8, FlatMap2/6, Match4/8, plain Log9 and All2 overhead28/30. QueueScope is outer Suspend + make WithFiber + returned Success + flatMap =4; framed decoration adds2. Offer16 includes outer Suspend, expression try2, flatMap1, Queue Suspend1, callback1, resumed Success1, and up to6 cancellation primitives (Sync/Success, combine OnFailure, flatMap, retained Failure, restoration Failure), plus three interruption/failure slots. Take16 covers outer Suspend, Queue Suspend, andThen continuation, callback, resumed Success, cancellation6 and spare interruption/terminal slots. Each additional wake/retry is charged16 more; framed Take adds2 decoration slots. Framed root interruption observers add20 per observed node as in DINT-005; group children retain framed decorations but omit those observers. No effects are executed by analysis, and payload metadata/layout is unchanged.

## Alternatives and validation

A fixed Take constant misses re-registration after barging. Counting unique IR identities misses shared occurrences. Observed scheduler maxima alone are corroboration rather than a proof. The global product is intentionally conservative; tighter per-fiber/turn bounds can follow real refused workloads, without silently widening the context assumptions.

Before delivery, test branch maxima/shared edges, strict saturation, context/input/cycle/unaccounted refusals, generated-profile refusal despite passing growth, and official plain/framed default-scheduler probes for transfer/backpressure, barging and blocked cancellation. Independently demonstrate actual automatic yielding on a longer source. Keep current generated debug/release parity, artifact/frames refusals and zero-cost/layout evidence intact. An independent primary-source audit reviews retry/cancellation reasoning. Native suites run serially.

## Delivered validation and review

The private selector now stores an immutable plain/framed receipt and refuses bounds reaching2048 after growth and trusted semantic checks. Forty tests across six source/reference suites pass. Official scheduler probes cover pressure at capacities1/2/3, barging, pending offer/take parent interruption, and the admission boundary. A longer Queue child independently triggers automatic yielding in both modes. Removing retry accounting fails the explicit receipt test; correct source is restored.
